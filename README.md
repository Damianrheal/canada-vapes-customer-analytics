# Canada-Vapes-customer-analytics
## About the Project
This project was completed through Riipen in collaboration with Canada Vapes. The main goal was to analyze customer purchasing behaviour and identify opportunities to improve customer value and support business growth. The analysis focused on areas such as average order value (AOV), repeat purchasing, customer retention, regional performance, and revenue contribution across Canadian provinces.
Using the available customer and order data, we carried out data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization to understand purchasing patterns and identify areas where the business could improve. A major focus of the project was Canada Vapes' goal of increasing AOV to $120 while making sure that recommendations were supported by the data rather than sales volume alone.
## My Role
As part of the project team, my main contributions focused on exploratory data analysis, developing and interpreting business-focused visualizations, and turning the findings into practical recommendations. I worked with customer and regional data to examine AOV, customer value, retention, and growth opportunities across provinces. I also contributed to the final reporting and presentation of our findings, including recommendations on customer retention, basket value, and where the business could focus its growth efforts.
## Key Skills & Tools
- **Power BI:** Developed business-focused visualizations to compare regional performance, customer value, AOV, and revenue contribution.
- **Python:** Used for data cleaning and preparing order-level data for analysis.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Used for descriptive statistics, data review, calculations, and initial exploration of key variables.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Examined distributions, outliers, purchasing patterns, customer behaviour, and regional differences.
- **Data Visualization:** Selected and designed charts to communicate findings clearly and answer specific business questions.
- **Business Analysis:** Connected analytical findings to the $120 AOV target, customer retention, basket value, and regional growth opportunities.
 ## Project Highlights
- Identified major differences in customer value across provinces, showing that high sales volume did not always mean stronger customer value or purchasing behaviour.
- Analyzed progress toward Canada Vapes' $120 AOV target and identified Nova Scotia as an important opportunity because of its large order volume and relatively small remaining AOV gap.
- Examined customer tenure alongside AOV to compare regional customer behaviour and identify provinces combining stronger customer relationships with higher order values.
- Used order-level data to explore repeat purchasing, customer retention, product purchasing patterns, and opportunities to increase basket value.
- Translated the analysis into practical recommendations focused on retention, increasing basket value, and testing regional strategies before scaling them.
